Strategy for a middle interval.

To find the middle \(p\) of a distribution, first compute the leftover area \(1 - p\text{,}\) then split it in half. Each tail gets \(\dfrac{1 - p}{2}\text{.}\)
🔗
The lower endpoint is at left area \(\dfrac{1 - p}{2}\text{.}\) The upper endpoint is at left area \(\dfrac{1 - p}{2} + p\text{.}\)
