Which bin gets the endpoint?

A bin includes its left-hand endpoint and excludes its right-hand endpoint.
🔗
So the bin labeled \(85\) to \(90\) contains every individual with a value of \(85\) or more, up to but not including \(90\text{.}\) An adult measuring exactly \(90\) cm belongs to the next bin.
🔗
Every histogram in this course follows this rule.
