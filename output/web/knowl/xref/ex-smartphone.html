Example 2.3.8. Ages of smartphone users.

In the United States, the ages of smartphone users aged 13 to 55+ approximately follow a normal distribution with mean \(36.9\) years and standard deviation \(13.9\) years.
🔗
(a) Find the probability that a randomly selected user is between \(23\) and \(64.7\) years old.
🔗
(b) Find the probability that a user is at most \(50.8\) years old.
🔗
(c) Find the 80th percentile and interpret it in a complete sentence.
🔗
Solution.
(a) normalcdf(23, 64.7, 36.9, 13.9) = 0.8186
🔗
(b) normalcdf(-1E99, 50.8, 36.9, 13.9) = 0.8413
🔗
(c) invNorm(0.80, 36.9, 13.9) = 48.6
🔗
The 80th percentile is \(48.6\) years. Eighty percent of smartphone users aged 13 to 55+ are \(48.6\) years old or younger, and twenty percent are \(48.6\) or older.
